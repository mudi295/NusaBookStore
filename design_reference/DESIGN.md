## Brand & Style
The design system manifests an intimate, literary sanctuary tailored for discerning mobile readers in Southeast Asia. Blending the deliberate composure of classic publishing with the fluidity of contemporary Android design, it evokes the sensory comfort of a boutique bookstore: fragrant paper, sun-dappled reading corners, and tactile book jackets. 

The aesthetic marries **Tactile Editorial** with **Soft Materiality**. Rich crimson tones anchor intellectual authority, while warm blush and rose tints offer hospitable softness. Layouts prioritize generous breathing room, high-contrast typography, and deliberate cataloging over frantic e-commerce patterns. Interactions feel weighted, graceful, and unhurried—celebrating the craft of storytelling.

## Colors
The palette balances intellectual depth with tender warmth through balanced chromatic layers.

### Light Mode Roles
- **Primary Maroon (`#800F2F`)**: Anchor color for key calls-to-action, navigation icons in active states, and headline links.
- **Deep Maroon (`#590D22`)**: Structural grounding used for top app bar titles, high-emphasis text accents, and footer areas.
- **Rose Pink (`#C9184A`)**: Expressive secondary for promotional tags, active filter pills, wishlist hearts, and secondary buttons.
- **Soft Pink (`#FFCCD5`)**: Gentle container fill for chip backgrounds, selected states, and quote highlight callouts.
- **Blush Canvas (`#FFF0F3`)**: Base viewport background that softens visual strain compared to sterile white.
- **Surface / Card (`#FFFFFF`)**: Elevated card containers, bottom navigation bar, and sheets to provide contrast against the canvas.
- **Text Primary (`#2B1720`)**: Deep plum-toned neutral providing AA/AAA compliant readability without harsh pitch-black contrast.
- **Text Secondary (`#806873`)**: Warm muted violet for metadata, author bylines, and subtitles.
- **Success Green (`#2E7D32`)**: Semantic indicator for in-stock statuses, successful cart updates, and digital delivery confirmations.
- **Divider / Outline (`#F1DCE2`)**: Soft rose-tinted hairline borders and structural rules.

### Dark Mode Adaptation
- **Background Canvas (`#180C12`)**: Deep nocturnal currant.
- **Surface Containers (`#28151F`)**: Elevated plum surfaces.
- **Primary Interactive (`#FF8FAB`)**: Luminous rose for high legibility on dark surfaces.
- **Secondary Interactive (`#FFB3C1`)**: Subtle soft rose for accents.
- **Text Hierarchy (`#FFF0F3` / `#D7B8C3`)**: High and medium emphasis tinted reading layers.

## Typography
Typography creates an intentional cadence between literary romance and mobile clarity.

- **Serif Roles (Newsreader)**: Reserved for evocative touchpoints: book titles, author showcases, editorial picks, quotes, and long-form book synopses (`body-lg`). Newsreader’s organic curves echo traditional book typography.
- **Sans-Serif Roles (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Handles microcopy, operational UI, navigation items, category filters, and numerical metadata.
- **Price Formatting**: All Indonesian Rupiah amounts use `Plus Jakarta Sans` bold styles formatted strictly with non-breaking spaces and period thousands separators (e.g., `Rp 85.000`, `Rp 145.000`), never lowercase or hyphenated.

## Layout & Spacing
Designed precisely around Android’s modern portrait viewport (393x852pt baseline), following a strict 4pt/8pt vertical and horizontal grid.

- **Screen Margins**: Outer canvas uses `1.25rem` (20px) horizontal padding to keep interaction targets well within mobile thumb boundaries.
- **Grid Structure**: Bookshelf product grids operate on a 2-column layout with a `1rem` (16px) gutter. Carousel rows bleed off the right screen margin by half an item width to signal horizontal discovery.
- **Touch Ergonomics**: All interactive tap areas (book cards, icons, buttons, list items) strictly observe a minimum height and width of 48x48dp.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth avoids synthetic, neutral drop shadows. Surfaces rely on **tinted ambient diffusion** and **tonal layering** that emulate soft ambient light on heavy matte paper stock.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: Blush background (`#FFF0F3`) acts as the foundation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Surface)**: Book cards and listing containers use pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a delicate rose-tinted border (`1px solid #F1DCE2`) and subtle warm ambient shadow (`0 4px 16px rgba(128, 15, 47, 0.04)`).
- **Level 2 (Floating Action & Bottom Bar)**: Bottom navigation bar, floating cart triggers, and filter bars sit on an elevated plane (`0 8px 24px rgba(89, 13, 34, 0.08)`) with a translucent blurred backdrop (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`).
- **Level 3 (Modals & Bottom Sheets)**: Checkout sheets and drawer menus rest above the canvas using a deep dimmed overlay (`rgba(43, 23, 32, 0.45)`) combined with `0 -8px 32px rgba(89, 13, 34, 0.16)`.
- **Book Cover Dimension**: Book cover assets display an internal micro-spine gradient on their left edge (`inset 2px 0 3px rgba(0,0,0,0.15)`) and a directional drop shadow (`2px 6px 12px rgba(43, 23, 32, 0.18)`) to mimic physical books.

## Shapes
Geometry is warm and inviting without feeling childlike. 

- **Primary Cards**: Book cards and modular showcase containers use `1.25rem` (20px) border radii for a pillowed silhouette.
- **Interactive Controls**: Buttons, text inputs, and search bars use smooth `0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px) radii.
- **Badges and Filter Chips**: Emphasize full pill curves (`9999px`) to create organic contrast against rectangular book jackets.
- **Book Covers**: Softened slightly with a `6px` radius to eliminate harsh digital corners while maintaining the structured appearance of printed cardstock.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: 48px height minimum. Filled with Primary Maroon (`#800F2F`), text in white (`#FFFFFF`, `label-lg`). Corner radius of 16px. Ripple animation tinted with Soft Pink.
- **Secondary Action**: Bordered with `1.5px solid #800F2F`, background transparent or `#FFF0F3`, text in `#800F2F`.
- **Wishlist / Floating Heart**: Circular 44x44dp button with a pure white surface, `#F1DCE2` border, and animated Rose Pink (`#C9184A`) heart icon.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Filter Chip (Default)**: `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #F1DCE2`, text in Text Secondary (`#806873`, `label-md`). Height 36px, fully pill-rounded.
- **Filter Chip (Selected)**: Filled with Soft Pink (`#FFCCD5`), border tinted `#C9184A`, text in Deep Maroon (`#590D22`, `label-md`). Includes an optional check or dismiss icon.

### Book Cards
- **Grid Card (Portrait)**: White container (`border-radius: 20px`, padding 12px). Features a 3:4 aspect-ratio book cover with physical edge shadow, book title in `Newsreader` (`title-md`, 2 lines max with ellipsis), author in `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`body-sm`, `#806873`), and price in `price-md` (`#800F2F`).
- **Badge Accents**: Editorial ribbon or pill placed over the cover corner (`Bestseller`, `Diskon 20%`, `Editor's Pick`) in Rose Pink (`#C9184A`) with white bold text (`label-sm`).

### Lists & Book Rows
- **Editorial List Item**: Horizontal card structure. Cover thumbnail on the left (72x96dp), right side stacking title (`headline-sm`), author, star rating with review count, stock indicator (`#2E7D32`), and primary Rupiah pricing. Separated by `1px solid #F1DCE2` dividers.

### Form Inputs & Search
- **Search Bar**: 52px height, pill or 16px radius, background `#FFFFFF` with `#F1DCE2` border. Leading search icon in `#806873`, placeholder "Cari judul buku, penulis, atau ISBN..." in `body-md`.
- **Form Text Fields**: White surface with subtle `#F1DCE2` border resting at 52px touch height. On focus, outline transitions smoothly to `2px solid #800F2F` with a soft rose glow.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Checkbox & Radio**: 24x24dp target nested within a 48dp touch footprint. Checked state fills with `#800F2F` featuring an inner white glyph. Unchecked states use a `2px solid #806873` outline.

### Indonesian Currency & Pricing Display
- Current price displayed prominently in `price-lg` (`#800F2F`). Strikethrough original prices sit adjacent in `body-sm` (`#806873`), followed by a Soft Pink tag displaying the percentage discount (e.g., `-15%`).
